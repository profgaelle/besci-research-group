## Description

GRACE (Gauging Resilience in the face of uncertAinty through Creative Engagement) asks whether people can become more able to engage productively with uncertainty, and whether that change can be observed in behaviour rather than inferred from questionnaire scores. The project brings together behavioural science, creative practice and computer vision.

This launch workshop brings together participants from judgment and decision-making, clinical, health and social psychology, neuroscience, organisational behaviour, creative practice, coaching and computer vision around three questions:

1. What changes as people become more able to engage productively with uncertainty?
2. What would count as evidence of that change?
3. How might that change show in people’s observable behaviour?

The workshop takes place online on **Friday 6 November 2026, 15:00 to 17:30 UK time** (16:00 to 18:30 CET, 10:00 to 12:30 US Eastern, 07:00 to 09:30 US Pacific). A link to the event will be sent to registrants before the meeting.

## How the workshop works

The workshop is interactive. Before the event, registrants can post the topics they would most like to discuss on a shared board, and the most popular topics set the agenda for the small groups.

The provocations will be recorded and, with the speakers’ permission, published on this page after the event. The small-group discussions will be transcribed for participants only, to support the writing of the workshop report. The plenary will not be recorded.

## Workshop report and the GRACE network

The discussions will feed into a workshop report setting out a shared working definition, which will be openly available. Participants can choose to become co-authors of the report, with each contribution recorded using [CRediT roles](https://credit.niso.org/), and to join the GRACE network to continue the conversation and collaboration.
